Palmshore is an original sun-bleached coastal crime game drawn entirely on a 2D canvas: ten by eight blocks of downtown towers, a beachfront motel strip, a working harbour, a palm park and low suburbs, all ringed by shimmering water and reachable over bridges. Ray Calderón comes home for his brother's funeral and finds the deed to the Sunray Hotel missing; five jobs with Rook, Marisol, Dunne and Vargas take him from a stolen car at Tide Line storage to a rooftop finale while the Palmshore P.D. escalates from a single cruiser to roadblocks and a helicopter. Walk with WASD, hold Shift to run, aim and fire with the mouse, press E to enter or leave a car (Space is the handbrake), R to rob a mark, 1–4 or Q to switch weapons, H for the horn and Esc for the pause/save menu; on the beach and in the city there is traffic to dodge, pedestrians to spook, pickups to collect and a courier run for extra cash.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
