# Palmshore

Palmshore is a complete, offline open-world crime game set in a sun-bleached Florida-style coastal city, drawn entirely with Canvas 2D and scored with WebAudio: a hand-built grid of six avenues and five boulevards runs through downtown, the beach strip, the docks, the suburbs and a palm-shaded park, and every street, building, palm, prop, car and person comes from one deterministic seed. Dee Marlow can walk, run, drive, carjack, shoot, brawl, rob passers-by and take five story jobs from Ivy, Sonny and the Gulls, with dialogue, cash rewards and a final score, plus courier runs, shops, health and armour pickups, traffic and pedestrians that panic when the shooting starts, a five-star wanted system with cruisers, officers, roadblocks and a helicopter, a minimap, a pause menu, local saves and synthesised sound.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open <http://localhost:8080>. Use WASD to move, the mouse to aim and fire, E to enter cars or use markers, Space for the handbrake or a punch, and Escape to pause. The four screenshots in `screenshots/` are captured from the game itself with `?shot=chase|boulevard|shootout|crane&seed=1`.
